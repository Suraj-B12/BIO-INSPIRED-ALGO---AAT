\chapter{Applications and Case Studies}
\section{Offline diagnosis and farming use}
The demo shows baseline and DE predictions side by side, their top-class scores, and Grad-CAM heat maps. Users can also add blur or noise to a leaf and watch the predictions change. A separate ``Evolution Lab'' animates how a DE population searches. These features suit classroom teaching and could support agricultural extension workers who advise farmers. Greenhouse monitoring, drone imaging, and disease-outbreak mapping are further possible uses; each would need new images and its own testing.

\section{Three test leaves in detail}
\Cref{fig:gradcam} shows the first test image of each displayed class, so the examples were not hand-picked. Both models classify all of them correctly. Grad-CAM shows which regions each model relied on~\cite{selvaraju2017gradcam}.

\textbf{Early blight, Leaf 2.} The leaf shows yellowing near the right edge. The baseline's heat map focuses on the lower edge and nearby background, while DE's strongest region covers much of the centre of the leaf. Both predict Early blight correctly; DE does so with lower confidence.

\textbf{Late blight, Leaf 3.} A discoloured patch runs along the left edge of the leaf. The baseline's attention sits close to that patch, while DE's map spreads more widely over the upper leaf and some background. Both predict Late blight correctly, using different image regions.

\textbf{Spider mites, Leaf 6.} The baseline's strongest attention includes background in the upper left and the leaf edge. DE highlights a larger part of the leaf. Both return the correct class, with different confidence levels.

The prediction gallery in the repository also shows DE's few remaining mistakes: Early blight taken for Late blight or Target Spot, and Late blight taken for Early blight. All of them involve the two blight diseases, matching the confusion matrix in Chapter~7.

\section{PlantVillage Nuru: a real-world example}
Mrisho et al. tested PlantVillage Nuru, a phone app that diagnoses cassava diseases offline~\cite{mrisho2020nuru}. They found that how images are taken, including how many leaves are examined, affects accuracy. This shows the practical points (phone, photo procedure, and user workflow) that matter when moving a tool like ours into the field.

\section{DE beyond this project}
DE is widely used in science and engineering, for example in design and energy problems~\cite{das2011survey,das2016recent}. DEHB combines DE with early stopping of weak trials for tuning~\cite{awad2021dehb}, and hybrid DE has been used to design CNNs~\cite{wang2018dearchitecture}.
