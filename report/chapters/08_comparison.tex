\section{Algorithm benchmarks and compute cost}
The median errors show landscape dependence. Classic DE has the lowest median on Rosenbrock, PSO on Sphere and Ackley, and jDE on Rastrigin and Griewank. Random search has the largest median error on each function. Thus successful convergence on Sphere does not establish superiority on multimodal functions or on the CNN task. Near-zero errors should be interpreted as numerical convergence rather than practically important decimal-level differences.

These tests are unadjusted for multiple comparisons and are not tie-corrected, although some recorded errors are tied. Their approximate $p$-values are descriptive exploratory outputs, not confirmatory significance claims. The interpretation rests on the recorded error magnitudes, distributions, and equal evaluation budgets; it does not generalize to every configuration of an algorithm family.
