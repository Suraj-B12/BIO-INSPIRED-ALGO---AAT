Baseline differences concern the entire recipe, including Adam versus AdamW; they are not gene ablations. DE versus random search isolates proposal strategy more directly.

There is one CNN search per method. Final training seeds measure variation conditional on selected recipes, not across independent searches. Analytic-benchmark replication does not apply to this comparison.

Published PlantVillage studies~\cite{mohanty2016plant,ferentinos2018plant,too2019plant} differ in labels and splits; evolutionary architecture studies~\cite{real2019evolution,awad2021dehb} differ in search spaces and resources. They provide context, not a directly comparable leaderboard.
